# Privacy Policy — Expense

**Effective date:** September 28, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

Expense is a personal expense-tracking app. All financial data you enter — transactions, wallets, budgets, and savings goals — is stored locally on your device using a local database. We do not collect or transmit this financial data to our servers.

## 2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up or export it yourself. We do not have access to your personal financial records.

## 3. How We Use Information

We do not use your financial data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than displaying it back to you within the app.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Expense uses the following third-party services to support paid subscription features:

- **Google Play Billing** — processes subscription payments through the Google Play Store.
- **RevenueCat** — manages subscription entitlements and verifies purchase status.

These services receive purchase-related information only (such as your subscription status and purchase receipt data). They do not receive your personal financial transaction data stored in the app. Data sent directly to these services is governed by their own privacy policies:

- Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 6. Data Deletion

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data through your device settings.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Expense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data processed through third-party services (Google Play Billing, RevenueCat), but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
